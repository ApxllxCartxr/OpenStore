Campaign Studio

The orchestrator drafts campaigns from your aggregated sales view and sends them here. Nothing reaches the public offer feed until you approve it with your passkey — review the signals it reasoned from, then approve or reject.

Register your passkey

One-time, per operator id. This registers a credential for — the same identity that approves campaigns below. If you've already registered for this operator id, you don't need to do this again.

Campaigns are drafted by openstore campaign draft, never in this page. Approving signs the campaign with a WebAuthn assertion bound to that campaign's id, so an approval cannot be replayed onto a different one.
